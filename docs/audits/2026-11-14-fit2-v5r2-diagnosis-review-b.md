## 2. Normative Domain Boundaries (Requirements & Active KB Evidence)

- **Authoritative policy rule ([`CHỦ ĐỀ 1.md §2.1–2.3, FR-02/FR-05`](../CHỦ%20ĐỀ%201.md#L48-L99)):** ML classifies all `46` Bitext intents; drafting covers `12` product intents (`11` draftable + `human_agent`), while the `35` non-draft intents (`34` `ESCALATED_34` + `human_agent`) escalate (`intent_policy.v2.json` `team_mapping`). Bitext `response` text is explicitly **non-authoritative** (`§2.3`); drafts must ground strictly in [`approved_kb.v2.json`](../../data/knowledge/approved_kb.v2.json) and abstain/escalate whenever KB lacks support (`FR-02`, `FR-03`, `FR-05`).
- **Store-return eligibility vs physical return channel (`return_policy` vs `return_product_in_store`):**
  - Raw Bitext (`994` rows each) contains `98/994` store-related queries inside `return_policy` and `964/994` store/shop/branch/in-person queries inside `return_product_in_store`.
  - SupportIQ's active KB (`POL-RET-001`, `POL-RET-002`, `POL-RET-003`) covers app return initiation, courier pickup, and *"designated Tiki partner post offices"* — **zero approved KB rules authorize brick-and-mortar retail store walk-in returns.** Therefore, physical-store return eligibility (`contrast_auth_pos_eval_102`: *"Is returning an item at a brick-and-mortar branch allowed?"*) belongs normatively to **`return_product_in_store` (`returns_support`, escalated)** so the system never fabricates ungrounded store-branch policy (`FR-02`/`FR-05`).
  - **Do NOT delete `82`–`87` `return_policy` eligibility queries** based on one failed case: general online return-eligibility questions (*"is it possible/eligible to return..."*) are core `return_policy` (`POL-RET-001`/`003`). Separate the physical-store channel cue from general eligibility at the **derived source-domain mapping/train contrast level**, not via blanket deletion, gold relabeling, or ad-hoc keyword rules.
- **Generic tracking vs live GPS (`track_order` vs `track_delivery`):**
  - Raw Bitext defines `track_order` (`988` rows, `ORDER`) largely around order/replacement status and `track_delivery` (`996` rows, `DELIVERY`) around generic *"where my shipment/package is"* phrasing (`1` store hit, virtually no GPS terms).
  - SupportIQ's active KB maps `track_order` to `POL-ORD-001` (order status + transit-hub tracking codes) and leaves `track_delivery` unmapped (`ESCALATED_34`, `delivery_support`; courier GPS verification sits in `POL-SHIP-004` under `delivery_issue`). Narrowing `track_delivery` in domain prompts to *"live GPS/courier van"* conflicts with Bitext's `263/270` generic `ambiguous_retained` `track_delivery` TRAIN rows, directly driving boundary instability (`final-case-07`: generic tracking predicted `track_delivery`).

  1. **Approve row-level derived taxonomy/mapping resolution in TRAIN only** (never editing eval/test gold or `final24`/`system156`/`noise420`): (a) add balanced train-only physical-store eligibility phrasing under `return_product_in_store` paired against online-only/general `return_policy` eligibility (keeping `82`–`87` non-store eligibility rows); (b) explicitly decide whether generic Bitext *"where is my package/shipment"* (`ambiguous_retained`, `263` TRAIN rows) remains `track_delivery` (`ESCALATED_34`) — accepting lower generic-tracking draft recall unless disambiguated by genuine order/replacement vs live courier-telemetry scope — or is aligned at derived source mapping before training.
